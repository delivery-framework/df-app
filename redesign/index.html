Why the old model is breaking

Traditional SDLC tools track activity. Fusion connects it to working software.

Requirements in Confluence. Work in Jira. Risks in a spreadsheet. Evidence assembled by hand before every release. It's the fragmentation, not the people, that's failing — in a Fusion engagement, your initiative runs on one record, so the trace holds from business intent to working software.
